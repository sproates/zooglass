## Boundaries and remaining acceptance

The evidence supports fresh-snapshot recovery from an abrupt local proxy break.
It does not establish replay of individual missed events, durable state,
completeness, atomic snapshots across views, silent-blackhole detection or a
production reconnect contract. Duplicate processing relies on the prototype's
sequential handling on one source connection, not global ordering across sources.

The final demo review still needs to reconcile fresh-checkout instructions,
shutdown/restart behaviour, keyboard and narrow-layout usability, capability
claims and the lasting architecture diagram. Existing tests support that review
but do not replace the complete walkthrough.

Test startup still invokes Maven through the service launcher. This review does
not resolve the separately recorded need to launch tests from prebuilt ZooGlass.
